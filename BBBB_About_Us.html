About Us:

Better Bathrooms by the Best Boys

Story of the Business:

In 2024, Baldivia and Baisa decided to create a project plant of bettering each and all public and private bathrooms as a school project. They eventually expanded this project and turned it into a fully fledged campaign website raising awareness to bathroom safety and cleanliness. They’ve noticed many public/private bathrooms being lower than cleanliness standard or safety of the consumers.

Now the 4B Business is currently at it’s peak as it’s currently being lead by CEO Boy, the same people who created the campaign. It has also expanded into a bathroom maintaining service and cobbled a suite of products essential in maintaining a clean bathroom.

Our Mission

We believe that in a Better Bathroom comes a Better Life as we aim to not only maintain but also improve the consumers overall experience. So not, only a success for us, it’s also a success for the consumers, a win-win situation, if I must say.
